### Problem Statement:
In sterile vial filling, air bubbles may arise due to multiple reasons. Air bubbles take different
forms depending on the cause. We aim to train a model that learns optimal parameters for
vial filling and is capable of mapping cause and effect. Furthermore, we would like to integrate
model suggestions as feedback into the manufacturing workflow.
